Part 3: UI & Score Canvas

Anchor Canvas to XR Rig

Making UI Follow the Player in VR

Why Anchor to XR Rig?

For UI elements that should follow the player's movement, the Canvas must be parented to the XR Rig's Camera Offset to maintain a consistent viewing position.

Keeps UI in player's field of view
Moves with player through the scene
Maintains comfortable viewing distance
Implementation Steps:
  1. Create Canvas with Render Mode: World Space
  2. Parent to XR Origin → Camera Offset
  3. Position at (0, 0, 1-2) in front of player
